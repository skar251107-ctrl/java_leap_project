About BloodLink

BloodLink helps people connect with eligible blood donors in their community through a searchable donor directory and clear donation history.

How it works

  1. Search donors by blood group and city.
  2. Contact available donors by phone or WhatsApp.
  3. Register as a donor to help your community find you.

Donor safety

Availability is calculated from the donor's last donation date. BloodLink applies a 90-day cooldown before marking a donor available again. Donors should confirm eligibility with a qualified medical provider.
